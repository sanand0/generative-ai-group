Maya: Antaripa shared Parlance Labs’ experiment on auto-evals, where they tested Braintrust, LangSmith, and Arize on masked traces.

Alex: So if you’re working on agents, don’t just ask “did it pass?” Ask “what kind of failure was this, and can I group similar failures together?”
Maya: That’s how you turn scattered bugs into something you can actually improve.
