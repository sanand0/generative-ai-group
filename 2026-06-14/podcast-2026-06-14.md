Alex: In other words, the benchmark is closer to how real teams judge code. That matters because as AI-generated code becomes normal, “works on my machine” is not enough.
Maya: The surprising takeaway is that we’re moving from “can the model solve the task?” to “can the model fit into a human production process?”
